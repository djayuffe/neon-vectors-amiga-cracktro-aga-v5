# Music / Paula

`assets/neon.mod` is a deterministic four-channel, 31-instrument `M.K.` ProTracker module generated by `tools/generate_assets.py`.

## v4 arrangement

The old four-pattern mechanical loop has been replaced by an eight-pattern scene arc:

1. establish — restrained bass, sparse lead and hats;
2. drive — rolling triad and regular rhythm;
3. lift — octave bass answers and denser hats;
4. peak — lead hook and end-of-pattern kick fill;
5. break — reduced bass/lead, softer dynamics and a rising hat rebuild;
6. rebuild — full groove returns;
7. peak variation — hook, accents and fill;
8. outro/loop — simplified groove that returns cleanly to pattern 0.

Harmony remains A-minor-centred Am–F–C–G so the intro retains its original identity. Channel roles are a rounded pulse/saw hybrid bass, additive harmonic lead, swept synthetic kick and fixed-seed decaying high-passed noise hat. Tonal samples use integer-period partials and whole-sample loops so their loop boundaries are deterministic and click-resistant.

The pattern data uses only semantics implemented by the compact replay: ordinary note/sample triggers, `Cxx` volume and `F01..F1F` speed. `F06` is written on the first row. The validator rejects unsupported commands rather than silently approximating them.

## Beat-synchronised graphics

At PAL 50 Hz and ProTracker BPM 125, one VBlank is one tracker tick. Speed 6 means six frames per row. The kick is placed every eighth row in the driving sections, giving a 48-frame downbeat period. `UpdateRaster` uses that same period for a three-frame white/cyan/blue Copper pulse at raster line `$80`; no audio-state introspection or IRQ coupling is required.

## Paula sample contract

All generated PCM is signed two-complement and centered on zero, matching Paula. The silence word is `$0000`. Percussion uses a one-word terminal loop that the player redirects to `audio_silence` after the trigger, preventing one-shot tails from repeating. For each trigger the player disables the channel DMA bit, loads location/length/period/volume, enables all prepared channels together, waits two raster lines, then installs the loop or silence terminal state.
